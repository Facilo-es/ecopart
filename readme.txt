Show the French éco-participation (DEEE, furniture) under prices, in the cart and on orders. Coupons never reduce it.

== Description ==

= Show your customers what they expect =

French shoppers are used to seeing "dont 2,20 € d'éco-participation" under the price at every big retailer. Ecopart puts that line on your product pages, cart, checkout, emails and orders, so your shop looks as trustworthy as theirs.

= Fewer mistakes on orders =

Each order line records the amount it was sold with, and coupons never reduce the fee, so the figures on your invoices match what you passed on. Change an amount next month and last month's orders stay exactly as they were.

= Set up in two minutes =

* A three-step wizard with a live preview of your product page, a phone cart and the order email.
* An amount field on every product and variation, with the amount including VAT worked out for you.
* Amount and fee type in Quick Edit.
* A sortable "Éco-part." column in Products, and a filter that shows which products still have no amount.
* A warning when a price is lower than its fee, so obvious mistakes don't reach your shop.

= Works with your shop =

* Classic and block cart and checkout, and the mini-cart.
* High-Performance Order Storage (HPOS).
* Any theme: the line takes your theme's font and colour.
* PDF invoice plugins that show item details.
* Prices shown with or without VAT, any tax class, and stores with taxes turned off.

= Also called écotaxe =

Ecopart shows the éco-participation, also called écotaxe, éco-contribution or éco-part DEEE, for electrical and electronic equipment, furniture, bedding and decoration textiles, and other products with an eco fee.

= Pro, for shops with supplier files =

Import your supplier's CSV or Excel file and update hundreds of amounts in a minute, with an undo button. Pro also adds amounts by category or weight, the total éco-participation on invoices and emails, free take-back at checkout, and a report by fee type. EUR 49 a year for one store, EUR 129 a year for up to 10 stores.

= What Ecopart isn't =

Ecopart shows the amounts you enter. It doesn't provide amounts and isn't legal advice. Check your amounts with your supplier or éco-organisme.

= Privacy =

Ecopart stores amounts in your own database and sends nothing anywhere. It adds no cookies and loads nothing from other sites.

= Security =

Please report security issues to info@facilo.es, and we treat every report as urgent. Questions are answered in this plugin's support forum on wordpress.org.

== Installation ==

1. Install and activate Ecopart from Plugins → Add New. WooCommerce must be active.
2. Open WooCommerce → Éco-participation and follow the three steps.
3. Open a product and fill in Éco-participation in the General tab. Enter a product price that already includes it.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Where do I get the amounts? =

From your supplier's invoice or file, which shows the amount for each product. If you bring products into France yourself, use your éco-organisme's barème (Ecosystem, Ecologic or Ecomaison, for example).

= Is the fee added on top of my price? =

No. Your price includes it, as in shops. Ecopart shows how much of the price is éco-participation and never changes the price or adds a fee line.

= Does it work with the block checkout? =

Yes. The line shows in the block cart and checkout, and orders placed through either checkout keep the same record.

= Can coupons reduce the fee? =

No. With coupon protection on (the default), a coupon only reduces the rest of the price, whatever its type, and the cart shows one short note saying so.

= Does it handle VAT? =

Yes, at the product's own rate. Enter amounts without VAT (HT), as in barèmes, or with VAT (TTC) if you prefer. Shops that show prices without VAT get the HT version of each line.

= Does it support furniture? =

Yes. Each product has a fee type: electrical and electronic equipment (DEEE), furniture (ameublement), or other.

= What happens to old orders if I change an amount? =

Nothing. Each order line keeps the amount, type and wording it was sold with.

= Comment afficher l'éco-participation dans WooCommerce ? =

Installez Ecopart, suivez les trois étapes dans WooCommerce → Éco-participation, puis saisissez le montant HT dans l'onglet Général de chaque produit. La ligne « dont … d'éco-participation » s'affiche sous le prix, dans le panier, la commande et l'e-mail. Le prix de vente doit déjà inclure l'éco-participation.
